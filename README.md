# Matrix-Lie-Algebras-Gauge-Theory

Supplemental notes developed for an undergraduate research presentation
at the Rutgers Aresty Research Center (2025–26), accompanying a co-authored
paper recasting Maxwell's equations as a U(1) gauge theory via differential forms.

Covers the general linear group GL(n,C) as a smooth manifold, its Lie algebra
under the commutator bracket, properties of the Hermitian conjugate, and the
derivation of the unitary group's Lie algebra as the anti-Hermitian matrices.


Written in LaTeX. Sole author.
